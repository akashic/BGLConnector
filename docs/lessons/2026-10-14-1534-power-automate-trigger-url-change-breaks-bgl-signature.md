When a Power Automate HTTP trigger URL is registered with a webhook provider that signs the exact URL it calls, a change to the trigger URL breaks both delivery and signature verification.

## What happened
BGL signs `timestamp + "POST" + fullUrl + rawBody`. The spike's inspector flow verifies against a copy of its own trigger URL stored in an environment variable, and that same URL is registered in BGL's Developer Portal. Microsoft's documentation shows trigger URLs have changed format. Old URLs stopped working on 30 November 2025, and from June 2026 a scale-unit segment (`/direct/cu/<n>/workflows/...`) can appear. The new URLs can also exceed 255 characters.

## Why it happened
The trigger URL is generated by the platform and isn't under the flow owner's control. Any change desynchronises three copies of it: the provider's registration (which needs the provider's approval), the stored verification URL, and the URL actually called.

## What to do instead
- Keep a fixed receiver, such as APIM or an Azure Function, as the registered URL, and hand off to the flow behind it.
- If a flow is registered directly, update the BGL registration and the stored URL together, and check that the provider's portal accepts URLs longer than 255 characters.
- Run `Get-AdminFlowWithMigratingTriggerUrl` to find flows with migrating URLs.

## How to recognise it early
Every webhook fails signature verification with "Signature mismatch" at once, or the trigger card shows a "copy the new trigger URL" banner.
